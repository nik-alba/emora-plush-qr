Program: Emora plush demo, run by Alba (Nikolas Baron Gobbato, sole proprietor, alba.cc). It is a product demo, not a live Emora Health service.

How you opt in

You scan the QR code on the demo plush toy, or open emora.lol/scan. Your phone opens a pre-written text to (231) 403-6672. You opt in by choosing to send it. We never text you first.

What you get

Scripted demo replies that walk through a sample therapist-matching conversation. Replies are sent only in answer to your texts. Message frequency varies, up to about 15 messages per conversation. There are no marketing messages.

Cost

The demo is free. Message and data rates may apply from your carrier.

Stop and help

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. You will get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or contact Alba through alba.cc.

Other terms

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. This demo gives no medical advice and books no real appointments. See the privacy policy for how information is handled.
