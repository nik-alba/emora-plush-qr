Text Messaging Terms

Emora plush demo text line · (231) 403-6672 · Last updated October 13, 2026

1. Program

The "Emora plush demo" is a conversational product demo run by Alba (Nikolas Baron Gobbato, sole proprietor, alba.cc). It is not a live Emora Health service. It gives no medical advice, matches you with no real provider and books no real appointments.

2. How you opt in

You opt in by scanning the QR code on the demo plush toy (or opening emora.lol/scan), reading the consent notice shown on that page, tapping Open Messages, and sending the pre-written text to (231) 403-6672 from your own phone. Sending that text is your consent to receive demo replies. We never send the first message, and we never add numbers from lists, forms or purchases.

3. What you receive

Scripted demo replies that walk through a sample therapist-matching conversation: a greeting, a few multiple-choice questions, three sample matches and a pretend booking confirmation. Replies are sent only in answer to your texts. Message frequency varies, up to 15 messages per conversation. There are no marketing or promotional messages.

4. Cost

The demo is free. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

5. How to opt out

Text STOP to (231) 403-6672 at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and nothing further. Texting STOP also ends any conversation in progress.

6. Help and support

Text HELP to (231) 403-6672 for help, or email nik@alba.cc.

7. Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for how your number and messages are handled. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

8. Other terms

  • Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • You must be 18 or older and the account holder or have the account holder's permission to use the demo on that phone.
  • Please do not send real health information. This demo is for demonstration only.
  • We may change or end the demo at any time.
