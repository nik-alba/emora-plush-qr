Privacy policy

Emora plush demo text line · (231) 403-6672 · Last updated October 8, 2026

This demo is run by Alba (Nikolas Baron Gobbato, sole proprietor, alba.cc). It shows how a parent could text a care team after scanning a QR code on a plush toy. It is not a live Emora Health service and no care is provided.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number and the text messages you send to the demo line.
  • When you scan the QR code: the practice code in the link, device type, language, time zone and approximate city.

How we use it

  • To reply to your texts with the scripted demo conversation.
  • To show the conversation on the demo's live view. Message text and the last two digits of your number appear there. Your full number does not.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party. Messages are carried by our SMS provider (Twilio) and the live view uses a message relay service (ntfy.sh), only to run the demo.

Do not send real health information

This is a demo. Please do not text real names, health details or other sensitive information.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help. To ask for your messages to be deleted, contact Alba through alba.cc.
