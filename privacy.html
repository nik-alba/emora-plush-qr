Privacy Policy

This demo text program is run by Alba (Nikolas Baron Gobbato, sole proprietor, alba.cc), referred to as "Alba", "we" or "us". It shows how a parent could text a care team after scanning a QR code on a plush toy. It is a product demo only. It is not a live Emora Health service, and no care, advice or appointments are provided.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number and the text messages you send to the demo line, including the pre-written opening text.
  • When you scan the QR code: the practice code in the link, device type, browser, language, time zone and approximate city. We do not store your IP address.

How we use it

  • To reply to your texts with the scripted demo conversation you asked for.
  • To show the demo conversation on the demo's live view. Message text and the last two digits of your phone number appear there. Your full number never does.
  • To honor STOP and HELP requests and keep a record of your opt-in and opt-out.

SMS consent and sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent or trade your phone number or messages. We share information only with the service providers that run the program on our behalf: Twilio (carries the text messages) and ntfy.sh (relays messages to the demo's live view). They may use it only to provide those services.

Retention

Messages are kept by our SMS provider for up to 30 days and in the live view for up to 12 hours. Opt-out records are kept so we never message you again after you reply STOP. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time (see below).

Do not send real health information

This is a demo. Please do not text real names, health details or other sensitive information. If you do, we will delete it on request.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help. To ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, email nik@alba.cc.
